01 / METHOD

Keep the geometry.
Give motion a metric reference.

Streaming visual models capture rich geometry. VIStream connects that geometry to calibrated visual–inertial motion—aligning coordinate frames, maintaining a persistent scale, and integrating valid motion updates into a camera trajectory.

01

Calibrate

Bring visual and inertial motion into the same camera frame, with one persistent metric scale.

02

Select valid motion

Check rotation and translation support across tracking epochs and interruptions.

03

Integrate & continue

Accumulate eligible motion. Use visual continuation when expert motion is unavailable.
